# Soldering Station Guide

## Setup

1. **Power on** the station
2. **Set temperature**: 350°C (660°F) for standard lead-free solder
3. **Wait for ready indicator** (usually a green light)
4. **Tin the tip**: Melt a small amount of solder on the tip before first use

## Basic Soldering Technique

1. **Clean surfaces**: Both surfaces should be clean and oxide-free
2. **Heat both parts**: Touch the iron to both the pad and component lead
3. **Apply solder**: Feed solder into the joint (not onto the iron)
4. **Remove solder, then iron**: Let it cool naturally
5. **Inspect joint**: Should be shiny, smooth, and volcano-shaped

## Good vs Bad Joints

| Good Joint | Bad Joint |
|------------|-----------|
| Shiny, smooth | Dull, grainy (cold joint) |
| Volcano shape | Ball shape (insufficient heating) |
| Solder flows onto surfaces | Solder blobs on top |

## Temperature Guide

| Solder Type | Temperature |
|-------------|-------------|
| Lead-free (SAC305) | 350-380°C |
| Leaded (60/40) | 300-330°C |
| SMD components | 320-350°C |

## Tip Maintenance

- **Clean frequently**: Use brass sponge after every few joints
- **Re-tin regularly**: Keep the tip coated with fresh solder
- **Never file tips**: This removes the protective plating
- **Replace when worn**: Black, pitted tips need replacement

## Safety

!!! danger "Burn Hazard"
    The tip reaches 350°C+ and stays hot for several minutes after power-off.

- Always return iron to holder
- Never touch tip or nearby metal
- Solder in ventilated area - fumes are harmful
- Wear safety glasses
- Tie back long hair
- No loose clothing or jewelry

## Cleanup

1. Power off station
2. Let cool completely (15+ minutes)
3. Store tips properly
4. Wipe down work area
5. Dispose of solder scraps in designated container
